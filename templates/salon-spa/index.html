The studio

Small on purpose

We book longer than most salons do, because rushing colour is how colour goes wrong. That means fewer clients a day and a calmer room, which most people notice within about ten minutes of sitting down.

Every new colour client starts with a consultation and a strand test. If what you have asked for will wreck your hair, we will tell you, and then we will suggest something that gets you close.

What to expect

Your first visit

  • A consultation before anything is mixed or cut
  • A strand test for every new colour client
  • A written price before we start, not after
  • Aftercare advice that does not require buying six products
  • A follow-up if colour needs adjusting within two weeks

Good to know

Before you come in

  • Come with your hair as it normally is, not freshly washed
  • Bring photographs, including ones you dislike
  • Tell us about any box dye, however long ago
  • Allow a little longer than quoted for a first colour
  • Patch tests are needed 48 hours before tinting
