Services

What we can do for you

Domestic and light commercial. If it carries water or gas, we can usually help.

Emergency plumbing

Phones are answered around the clock by an engineer, not an answering service. We will talk you through shutting off a supply before we set off if that stops the damage getting worse.

  • Burst and leaking pipes
  • Total loss of heating or hot water
  • Blocked drains and toilets
  • Leaking cylinders and tanks
  • Gas leaks and unsafe appliances

Boilers and heating

We fit Worcester, Vaillant and Ideal, and we will tell you which makes sense for your house rather than which carries the best margin.

  • Boiler supply and installation
  • System and combi conversions
  • Powerflushing and system cleaning
  • Radiator replacement and balancing
  • Smart thermostat installation

Bathrooms

Full fits including tiling and electrics through trusted trades we have worked with for years. One point of contact and one price.

  • Complete bathroom installation
  • Shower and wet room fitting
  • Tiling, flooring and extraction
  • Accessible and walk-in adaptations

Landlords

Annual certificates with automatic reminders, so nothing lapses and nothing gets forgotten between tenancies.

  • Gas safety certificates (CP12)
  • Annual boiler servicing
  • Void property checks and drain-downs
  • Multi-property contracts and priority response
