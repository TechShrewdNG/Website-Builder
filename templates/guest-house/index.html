See the rooms

The house

Built in 1841 and still creaking in the right places

We bought it in a state, spent four years putting it right, and kept everything worth keeping. The floors slope, the windows are original, and the beds are new because there are limits to authenticity.

There is no lift, no restaurant and no evening service. What there is: a fire in the snug, a shelf of paperbacks people have left behind, and someone downstairs who knows where the tide tables are.
