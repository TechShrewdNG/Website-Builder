What we do

Practice areas

Four things, done properly. We refer out anything outside them rather than learn on your file.

Corporate and commercial

Most of our corporate work is unglamorous and preventative: getting the shareholder agreement right while everyone still likes each other. We also handle acquisitions and disposals up to around the mid-market.

  • Shareholder and partnership agreements
  • Share and asset purchases
  • Joint ventures and investment rounds
  • Supply, distribution and terms of business
  • Company restructures and reorganisations

Commercial property

Acting for landowners, developers and occupiers. We are comfortable with conditional contracts, overage and the kind of title problems that surface late in a transaction.

  • Freehold acquisitions and disposals
  • Development and option agreements
  • Commercial leases and renewals
  • Landlord and tenant disputes
  • Secured lending and refinancing

Employment

We act for employers. That means contracts and handbooks when things are calm, and settlement agreements or tribunal defence when they are not.

  • Contracts of employment and handbooks
  • Redundancy and restructuring
  • Settlement agreements
  • Disciplinary and grievance process
  • Employment tribunal defence

Dispute resolution

Litigation is expensive and slow, and we will tell you when it is not worth it. When it is, we run it tightly and keep you informed about cost at every stage.

  • Contract and warranty disputes
  • Shareholder and partnership disputes
  • Professional negligence claims
  • Debt recovery
  • Mediation and negotiated settlement
