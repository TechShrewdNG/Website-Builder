Photography

Most homes sell or fail on the first three pictures

Every listing gets a professional shoot, shot at the time of day the light actually works in that room, plus a measured floorplan. We do not use a phone, and we do not use the wide-angle lens that makes a bedroom look like a ballroom.

If the weather is wrong on the day, we come back. It costs us a morning and it is worth it.
