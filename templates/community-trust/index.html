Why we work this way

Not a service you are referred into and processed by

People come to the food club because it is cheaper than the shops, and while they are there they find out that the advice desk can sort the housing letter they have been ignoring for a month. That only works because it is the same room, the same day, with people they already recognise.

We are deliberately small. We cover one ward, we know the schools and the surgery by name, and we would rather do three things properly than nine badly.

Our three programmes

What actually happens in the hall

The food club

A weekly shop for a membership fee, not a parcel handed to you. Members choose what goes in the bag.

Advice desk

Benefits, housing and debt. Two trained advisers, no appointment needed, and we will come to the meeting with you.

Repair workshop

Bring the broken lamp, the bike, the coat with the missing zip. Someone here can probably fix it with you.

Volunteering

Two hours a fortnight is genuinely useful

  • Food club — unpacking, stocking and serving, Wednesdays
  • Repair workshop — no expertise required, someone will show you
  • Advice desk — training provided, six-month commitment
