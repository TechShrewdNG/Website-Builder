In more detail

How each one works

The food club

Members pay five pounds and choose roughly twenty pounds of shopping. Stock comes from surplus redistribution and from what we buy to fill the gaps, because surplus never includes nappies or washing powder.

Advice desk

Two advisers with proper training in benefits, housing and debt. They will read the letter, explain it, write the reply, and sit next to you at the assessment if you want them to.

Referrals

If you work at the school or the surgery

You do not need to fill anything in. Tell the family the hall is open on Wednesday and that they can just turn up. If it would help for one of us to meet them first, ring and we will arrange it.

We do share anonymised numbers with our funders, and nothing else. Nobody's name leaves the building.
