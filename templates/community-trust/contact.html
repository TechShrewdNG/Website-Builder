The hall

Fieldgate Community Hall, Ardley Road, next to the health centre. Step-free entrance at the side, and the number 12 stops outside.

Donating goods

Tinned food, toiletries and washing powder are always short. Please do not bring clothes, we have nowhere to put them and no way to sort them.
