What we run

Three ways to train

Barbell Club

Squat, press, deadlift. Technique first, then load. The programme that most people should start with and never really outgrow.

60 min — max 8 people

Conditioning

Hard, short and scalable. Rowers, bikes, sleds and carries. Built to leave you tired, not broken.

45 min — max 12 people

One to one

For a specific goal, a specific sport, or coming back from injury. Programmed between sessions, not made up on the day.

60 min — by arrangement

The gym

Eight racks, good bars, enough plates

We spent the fit-out budget on equipment rather than a smoothie bar. What you get is a big open floor, kit that works, and someone who knows your name and your numbers.

  • Eight competition racks and platforms
  • Calibrated plates and a full dumbbell rack to 50kg
  • Sleds, sandbags, rowers, bikes and a turf strip
  • Changing rooms, showers and secure lockers
  • Open 5.30am to 9.30pm on weekdays
