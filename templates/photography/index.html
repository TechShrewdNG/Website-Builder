How I work

Present, but not in the way

The best photographs from a day are almost never the ones anybody asked for. My job is to be close enough to catch them and quiet enough that they still happen.

  • Mostly film, digital where the light demands it
  • No second shooter unless the day genuinely needs one
  • Full gallery delivered within four weeks
  • Every frame edited by hand, none by preset
  • Negatives archived and kept indefinitely
