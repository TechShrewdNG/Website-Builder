About

I photograph people as they are

Fifteen years behind a camera, the last nine of them freelance and mostly on film.

I came to this from press work, which is where the habit of staying out of the way comes from. I do not arrange people, I do not interrupt, and I will never ask a room full of guests to do something again for the camera.

Most of what I shoot is medium format black and white, developed at home. Digital comes out when the light disappears, which on the west coast is often.

Process

How a booking works

  • A conversation first, in person or on a call
  • A written quote covering hours, travel and delivery
  • A third up front to hold the date
  • A pre-shoot call two weeks before
  • Full gallery within four weeks of the day
  • Negatives archived and available indefinitely

Practical

Common questions

  • International commissions are welcome, costs at actual
  • Print files and albums are ordered separately
  • Weddings are capped at eighteen a year
  • I hold one provisional date at a time, free, for a fortnight
