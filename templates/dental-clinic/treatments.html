Paying for it

Plans, finance and the honest version

Our monthly plan covers two check-ups and two hygiene visits a year and takes ten per cent off everything else. It works out cheaper than paying as you go if you actually come twice a year, and worse if you do not, which is roughly the whole story.

For larger work we offer interest-free finance over twelve months. We will show you both numbers and let you pick.
